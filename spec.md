# Wolfpaw
### Tread lightly.

Wolfpaw is an agentic personal assistant or intelligent employee who can follow instructions, learn, and create. You control what data you want your Paw to have access to and how you want to talk to your Wolf (ie. by Telegram, email, text, voice, etc.)

Wolfpaw can learn from resources you tell it about or give it directly (by giving a link to a web resource or by uploading documents to its folder). As it learns to do things, it can... do them! For example, watch something and store information about it for analysis. Store data over time so it can see trends and help with suggestions when you need. You could give it access to your activity monitor and tell it what you like to eat if you want help with fitness goals, or have it watch different political indicators if you like current events, or watch market data if economics and invenstment is a passion. Set reminders, get help with cooking. Give it your tax documents to see if you can save any money. Upload all of your businiess receipts and have it do your bookkeeping, or marketing, or inventory. All these things can be trained.

## How does it do that?
It's a big-ass harness, very capable. There are several steps to processing a user query. Some get deeper analysis and tools than others. Simple questions are processed by looking something up and returning a well-formated reply. Other things go into a planning phase using a better or best model to figure out how to accomplish complex tasks.

### Triage Agent
- Put the goal together into an idea with a specific outcome.
- send that to the right agent:
    - simple agent, makes a call and returns the answer
    - Complex task agent, uses a better model, does its own triage.

### Planning Agent
- Triage step
    - is it a simple or complex task
    - What does the user really want?
    - Create a list of outcomes, the goal of the session
- Planning step
    - Check procedural memory:
        - have we done this before?
        - did it work?
        - take the good bits of what was found for reference
    - Figure out what steps are needed to complete the task
        - break the task town into broad steps
        - search for relevant tools and skills that could help
        - assemble them into a plan
    - Refine plan step
        - figure out what is missing from the plan, what could break, what are the weak points?
        - If things are missing, figure out how to work around the issue
            - answer found? put it in the plan
            - answer not found, see if you can create, invent, or invoke something to get what you need. Use some creativity, don't cause trouble.
            - ask the user for their input, their workaround, their plan B
